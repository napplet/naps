# NNNN — Decision title

**Recorded:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Status:** proposed / existing direction documented / accepted / superseded
**Source revision:** repository and immutable revision
**Review:** linked PR or discussion; mark pending when none exists
**Supersedes / superseded by:** linked record or none

## Context

State the concrete problem, affected actors, and boundary or compatibility risk.
Identify the owning NAP, convention, NAAT, or projection. Link authoritative
sources; distinguish observations from assumptions.

## Decision

State the chosen direction and where its requirements live. For a proposal,
state what remains subject to maintainer review. For a retrospective record,
describe the established direction without inventing a new approval.

## Alternatives

| Alternative | Benefit | Cost or boundary conflict | Evidence |
|-------------|---------|---------------------------|----------|
| Concrete option | What it makes easier | What it weakens or complicates | Linked source or explicitly labeled inference |

## Consequences

Describe effects on user choice, authority, interoperability, implementation
effort, dependencies, and migration. Keep relevant residual risks visible.

## Verification and reconsideration

Name the observable checks and available implementation evidence. Mark absent
evidence as absent. State what new evidence or changed requirement would justify
reopening the decision.

## References

- Owning requirement and immutable source revision
- Actual review or implementation evidence
